Welcome to the Nigerian Community Continuation Petition.

This platform is for Nigerians living in The Gambia who wish to support the continuation of a united, inclusive and representative Nigerian Community in The Gambia, with the participation and collective interests of Nigerians and their ethnic communities.

Signing this petition is your voice for the Nigerian Community in The Gambia. Your signature demonstrates your support and helps make the collective voice of Nigerians heard.

Please provide your accurate information and sign the petition only once. Your information will be recorded as part of the petition support database.
